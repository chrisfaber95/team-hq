# Vision

**Product:** My Game Project (`N:\Projects\MyGameProject`): Unreal Engine 5.8. A PC remake of
*Indiana Jones and the Infernal Machine* (LucasArts, 1999), a 3rd-person puzzle/shooter, rebuilt with
a free-toggle 1st/3rd-person camera.

## Who it's for
Myself. A personal and learning project; it is not distributed.

## The problem it solves
The 1999 original is dated: awkward controls, fixed 3rd-person camera, hard to run on a modern PC.
I want to play it again, and to learn how a full game is built by the agent team.

## What it should become
A faithful rebuild of the original: same chapters, levels, puzzles, enemies and items, with modern
controls. The player can switch between 1st and 3rd person at any moment, and every ability (aim, whip,
climb, puzzles) works in both. First milestone is one complete level (Babylon dig site) as a playable
vertical slice, then the rest of the campaign level by level.

## Principles
- Level layout, puzzle logic and pacing follow the original (1:1); controls and camera are modern.
- Both camera views are first-class: no ability may work in only one.
- No assets extracted from the original game files. Greybox/placeholder first, free or own assets later.
- Every level is playable start to finish before the next is started.

## Not the goal
Publishing or distributing the game; multiplayer; new story or new levels (for now).
