# Indiana Jones and the Infernal Machine (1999): reference note

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_Jones_and_the_Infernal_Machine (read 2026-10-17)

- Third-person, camera behind Indy; only HUD element is a health indicator bottom-left.
- Verbs: run, jump, climb, bullwhip (platforming/swing), firearms, whip, machete; puzzles and treasure hunting are the focus.
- Some levels have vehicles: rafting, jeep, mine cart.
- Goal: collect four machine parts for the Infernal Machine (Tower of Babel engine); 17 levels in total.
- Hint system; "Indy Quotient" score returns. PC controls were the most criticised part.
- Locations in plot order: Canyonlands dig site, Babylon dig site, Kazakh monastery, Palawan volcano, Olmec valley, Meroe tomb, Babylon (Room of the Tablets), Aetherium, bonus Peruvian temple level.
- Plot (1947): Indy and Sophia Hapgood chase Soviet physicist Volodnikov; the god Marduk is behind the machine; Turner (CIA) betrays them; Indy defeats Marduk.
- The page gives no ordered list of all 17 levels and no save-system details: the user must supply level reference.
