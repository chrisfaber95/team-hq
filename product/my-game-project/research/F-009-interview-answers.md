# F-009 interview answers (user, 2026-10-17)

Asked by the lead; the designer turns these into the revised F-009 brief and asks the user to approve.

- View swap: smooth glide, about 0.3 s, player keeps control during the blend.
- 3rd-person facing: character faces its move direction normally (free-run); faces the camera direction when aiming or using the whip.
- 1st person: full body visible (own legs and shadow), camera in the head. Needs criteria for no clipping and for the body not blocking the view.
- Input: keyboard+mouse and gamepad.
- Movement feel: modern and responsive (snappy acceleration, air control, coyote time, jump buffering). Not 1:1 with the original's speeds.
- Moves in this brief: walk, run, jump, crouch, roll/dodge, sidestep/strafe.
- 3rd-person camera: over-the-shoulder, free orbit with mouse/right stick, pulls in near walls.
- In-game settings now: FOV (separate value per view), look sensitivity. Not now: invert Y, key rebinding.

Follow-ups for the brief: test map needs a low-ceiling crouch case and room for a roll; decide whether sidestep means lock-on strafing or lateral movement; the "equal speed in both views" criterion stays.
